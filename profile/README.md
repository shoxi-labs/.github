### 📱 TypeTalk

🇭🇷 Android aplikacija koja gluhim i nagluhim osobama olakšava razgovor licem u lice: napišete poruku, a ona se prikaže velikim slovima, okrenuta prema sugovorniku. Bez računa, bez interneta, bez oglasa. *Uskoro na Google Playu.*

🇬🇧 An Android app that makes face-to-face conversation easier for deaf and hard of hearing people: type a message and it appears in large letters, facing the other person. No account, no internet, no ads. *Coming soon to Google Play.*
